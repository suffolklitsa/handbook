# The rules

These apply in every LITSA repo, with or without an AI agent. Each repo's `AGENTS.md` repeats them along with its own rules.

1. **No secrets.** Keys, tokens and passwords never go in a file, a commit, an issue or a test. If you find one, tell a maintainer; deleting it in a later commit isn't enough, because it stays in the history.
2. **No personal data.** No real names, emails, student records, sign-in sheets or mailing lists in any repo. Use made-up examples.
3. **No invented law.** Every legal statement has a source you can link. Never make up a citation.
4. **A person checks every fact.** Guides, summaries, requirements and case materials are confirmed by a person against the real source, and the ones labeled `needs R&W review` by a second person from Research & Writing.
5. **Small changes.** One issue per pull request.
6. **Run the checks** and report what they actually printed.
7. **Disclose AI use** in the pull request description. You own the work either way.
8. **Mind course policies.** Never use an agent for graded work where your course says no.
9. **Don't paste private material** (the challenge case file, the assistant's private repo) into tool accounts other people can see.
